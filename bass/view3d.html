In the view: turn with one finger, zoom or move with two (mouse: drag, right-drag or Shift-drag, wheel); double-tap (double-click) a surface to make that point the centre of turning and zooming; tap a key to press it. Scroll the page outside the view. The arrow and ＋/－ buttons work too; Reset view goes back to the start. Ortho switches to a parallel projection with a mm grid behind the model (1 mm, every 10th bold, coarser when zoomed out; its pitch in the corner); +X … −Z look from that side of the bass, in Ortho. The key deck on top: a thin tray round one Hall-sensor board under both rows, a cover with the keys' tubes and a 10 mm step for the back row, the hand plate's top flush with the cover; below it a thin closed box (11.2 mm), 211 mm wide, one rectangular block with the deck (stored, the discant's outer keys stay uncovered), ribbed inside for a stiff hand plate, closed at the back by a cover that slides in from the −x end, sits on a 45° ramp at its bottom and is screwed at its top; the hand plate (131.5 × 128.3 mm, from the earlier BassCase, half as wide and 25 % lower) is its flat front; the earphone jack sits in the +x side wall, in a bulge of the back cover. Inside, a 1S battery (dark, with the PCBs). In the step under the deck, behind the box, the gauges' inner holders are printed with the case, on the discant's gauge plates (grey). Every printed part (case, back cover, cover) has its own button; turn the Case off to see inside. Press moves the front row's middle key 3 mm until its O-ring lands on the tube's top and back (with All keys on, every key; tap any key in the view to press it); the springs are left out, hidden under the caps' skirts. Under the deck behind the box hangs the short socket for the stored discant; the box is long enough below the hand plate for the discant's whole thumb bar to slide past the mount. All parts come from the OpenSCAD build (switch_hall/, hall_pcb/ and bass/).
