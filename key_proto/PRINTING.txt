KEY PROTOTYPE - PRINTING ON A BAMBU LAB X2D
===========================================

A test piece of 8 button keys for an electronic button accordion (a Steirische
Harmonika without bellows): a tray, a thin board and a cover with the keys' guide tubes,
plus 8 key caps and 8 small collars. Everything is PETG.

FILE
----
  plate_x2d.3mf      all parts for one prototype, one plate     about 1 h 30 min, 35 g PETG

A finished Bambu Studio project: printer, filament and settings are set, the parts
already lie the right way up and are placed on the plate.

HOW TO PRINT
------------
1. Open the file in Bambu Studio (File > Open Project, or drag it in).
2. Check the top left: printer "Bambu Lab X2D 0.4 nozzle", filament "Bambu PETG HF"
   (another PETG is fine: pick its profile), plate "Textured PEI Plate".
3. Press "Slice plate", then "Print plate" (or export to SD card).
   Always slice again before printing: the files were made by Bambu Studio's command
   line, and slicing in the program completes them.
4. Plate: textured PEI. PETG sticks too well to smooth PEI and can tear it.

Settings already in the files (in case you need to redo them):
  0.16 mm layers, 3 walls, 5 top and 5 bottom layers, 25 % gyroid infill,
  no support, no brim (add a brim only if the small collars come loose).

Do not rotate or turn the parts: each one lies on the face it must be printed on.
The tubes on the cover lean slightly on purpose.

WHAT TO EXPECT
--------------
  1 tray       103 x 37 mm, 4.5 mm high, open on top, with small posts inside
  1 board      100 x 34 mm, 1.6 mm thin plate with 5 holes
  1 cover      103 x 37 mm plate with 8 tubes about 13 mm high
  8 caps       15 mm across, 9.5 mm high, printed upside down (flat top on the plate)
  8 collars    4.4 mm across, 3 mm high, very small - don't lose them
