Tap Interact, then turn with one finger and zoom or move with two (mouse: drag, right-drag, wheel); tap it again to scroll the page. The arrow and ＋/－ buttons work too. Press moves the middle key of the front row 3 mm until its O-ring lands on the tube's top and back; Hold down keeps it there; with All keys on, both act on every key. With Interact off, tap or click any key to press it. The board is drawn with its parts' bodies (boxes, from the KiCad board); the springs are left out, hidden under the caps' skirts. All parts come from the OpenSCAD build (switch_hall/, discant/, hall_pcb/).
