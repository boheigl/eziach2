Hall-sensor key with the spring under the cap

A slice of the discant staircase (four boards, each 10 / 16 / 3 mm up from the last) with the low key. Everything that moves is above the board: a plunger in a tube on the cover, a stock spring round the tube, hidden under the cap's skirt, and the magnet in the plunger's foot. The Hall sensor sits under the board, exactly on the key's axis, and the board has no key holes.

Force and sensor

Key force

Linear over the 3 mm (stock spring ⌀8 × 0.4 × 25). Soft stops at both ends: an O-ring at the bottom, a silicone washer at rest.

Field at the sensor, on the axis

Monotonic, not linear. The firmware stores each key's rest and bottom reading and maps the reading back to travel, so the actuation point is a travel, not a field value.

Notes
  • Parameters are those of key.scad, copied at the top of this page's script. Change them in both places.
  • Guide. The plunger is guided at two points: its collar in the tube's lower bore and its pin in the upper bore, 4.5 mm apart at rest. Jamming would need a press 9 mm off centre with a friction of 0.25, which is outside the ⌀15 cap. The step between the bores keeps the plunger captive: it goes in from below before the cover is screwed onto the board, and the cap is pressed on afterwards.
  • No click. Both stops are soft. At the bottom, a stock O-ring (4 × 1.5, soft silicone) lies in a 0.5 mm counterbore under the cap and lands on the tube's top; it also centres the spring's top coil. At rest, a washer punched from 1 mm silicone or PU sheet (⌀4.5 / ⌀3.5, sitting slightly tight on the pin) bears on the step in the bore.
  • The spring is hidden. A skirt under the cap (⌀12.4, one piece with it) slides over a shroud on the cover (⌀10.2) without touching it. At rest they overlap by 1 mm, so the spring is never visible. At the bottom the skirt stays 0.3 mm above the cover. This costs no height. Turn the section on to see the spring.
  • Sensor through the board. The sensor on the underside leaves the plunger free to come down to 0.3 mm above the board. The magnet is 2.5 mm from the sensor element at the bottom and 5.5 mm at rest.
  • The section cuts at x = 20, the axis of the pressed key on the lowest board. Drag to orbit; scroll or pinch to zoom.
  • Not modelled yet: the rest of the cover (walls, screws), the sensor tracks, and the ADC or MCU on each board.
